FAQ

Questions teams ask before their health check.

30 minutes with a solution architect, not a salesperson.

Do you work with brand-new HubSpot accounts?

Yes. We implement HubSpot from scratch as well as fixing existing portals. If you’re about to buy HubSpot, also see our onboarding page, where HubSpot’s onboarding fee can be waived.

Can you fix a portal another partner set up?

Yes, it’s one of the most common reasons teams come to us. We keep what works, fix what doesn’t, and document everything so you’re never left guessing again.

Will we lose any data?

No. We back up your portal before any change, test every fix on sample records first, and switch fixes on only after you approve them.

Can our team keep using HubSpot while you work?

Yes. Everything is built and tested in the background. Nothing changes for your team until you approve it.

What happens on the free health check?

A 30-minute call with a solution architect about your goals and your current setup. Within 24 hours you get a written fix list and a fixed-price plan, yours to keep either way.

How do we know which tier we need?

You don’t have to. Pick the closest one, and we confirm it on your health check before you commit to anything.

What if you find more problems once you start?

Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted before we touch it. Your price never changes without your approval.

Do you handle integrations?

Yes. We have a dedicated in-house integrations team. Native integrations are included, and custom integrations are $500 each.

Can you lower our HubSpot bill?

Often, yes. We review paid seats, marketing contacts and AI credits so you only pay for what you actually use.

Who will actually work on our portal?

Growlyze’s own team. Your solution architect leads from the first call, backed by our implementation, integrations and AI specialists.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Plans start at $500 a month, with no minimum term, and unused hours roll over forever.
